PASSENGER COMFORT INTELLIGENCE

Passenger comfort,
measured in motion.

AHENK is a research-oriented iOS platform for recording real-world journey context, iPhone motion-sensor measurements, and participant-reported discomfort events.

MOBILITY RESEARCH PLATFORM

Built for structured journey observation.

AHENK links motion traces with passenger context and time-stamped reported events while keeping the current version focused on descriptive research outputs.

Motion capture

Records timestamped accelerometer and gyroscope observations during an active journey.

Passenger-reported events

Allows discomfort events to be marked during the journey and retained with the session record.

Journey archive

Stores completed sessions locally with duration, context, event counts, and descriptive motion summaries.

Privacy by current design

Research data remains on the device.

In AHENK 1.0, research recordings are stored locally on the iPhone. The app does not provide cloud synchronization, remote backup, advertising, or third-party analytics.

Research-use limitations

Descriptive, not diagnostic.

AHENK is intended for research use. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis, treatment recommendations, validated clinical outcomes, a Comfort Index, or causal interpretation.
