AHENK Support

Support

Support for AHENK Research version 1.0.

Contact

For technical questions or App Store support, email caglaaydin.96@hotmail.com.

Local data

Research recordings are stored locally in the current version. Use Settings → Reset Local Research Data to permanently delete them.

No sign-in required

AHENK 1.0 does not require an AHENK username or password to record and review local journeys.

Before contacting support

  • Confirm that the installed app is the latest App Store version.
  • Restart the app if a screen does not refresh as expected.
  • Do not delete local research data unless you intend to permanently remove stored records.

Research-use note

AHENK is intended for research use and descriptive observation. Support requests cannot provide medical advice or clinical interpretation.
