Terms of Use

Effective date: 19 August 2026 • AHENK version 1.0

1. Purpose

AHENK is a research-oriented application for recording and descriptively summarizing journey-context information, device motion-sensor data, and participant-reported discomfort events.

2. No medical service

AHENK is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, clinical decision support, motion-sickness risk prediction, or validated clinical outcomes.

3. Research responsibility

Users and researchers remain responsible for participant information, informed-consent procedures, ethics or institutional approvals where applicable, lawful research conduct, safe device placement, data interpretation, and compliance with relevant requirements.

4. Safe use

Do not interact with AHENK in a manner that distracts a driver or compromises road safety. Device placement and interaction during travel must be carried out safely and lawfully.

5. Data and backups

AHENK 1.0 stores research recordings locally on the device and does not provide remote backup for those records.

6. Availability and changes

Features may be modified, added, or removed in future versions. Future versions may be governed by updated terms and privacy information.
